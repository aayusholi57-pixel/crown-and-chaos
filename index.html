HOW TO CONQUER

  1. Choose an army size: 10, 50, or 100 soldiers, then select a troop class.
  2. Deploy your army. Soldiers automatically form ranks inside your kingdom.
  3. Both armies move and fight automatically. Optional: click one of your soldiers, then click a destination to override its movement.
  4. Blue deploys its army in the Azure territory. Click BEGIN THE INVASION, or use Quick Battle to skip setup.
  5. Both armies advance, engage nearby enemies, and attack the opposing stronghold. Destroy the enemy castle to win.

BATTLEFIELD COMMAND: select an individual soldier and click a destination. Troops can enter either kingdom. During battle, units automatically seek nearby enemies, fight in formation, and besiege the enemy castle. Soldier, Archer, Knight, and Mage formations have different movement speeds. A stronghold reaching zero integrity collapses and ends the battle.
